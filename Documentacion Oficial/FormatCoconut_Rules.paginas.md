Rules for [Format Coconut] Beta
[Format Coconut] is a thematic multiplayer format for playing the Disney Lorcana Trading
Card Game. You play in a group, with each deck built around a favorite character!
• First, use the app to choose a [Coconut] card. Each one has special abilities to use
as inspiration for your deck and is associated with a specific Disney Lorcana card.
• Then, choose up to three ink types for your deck. One of them must be the same as
your [Coconut] card’s ink type.
• Next, build a deck around your [Coconut] card with at least 60 cards.
• Your deck can have up to 4 copies of the Disney Lorcana card associated with your
[Coconut] card. You can’t have more than 1 copy of any other card, though.
For example, say you choose the Sapphire [Coconut] card Moana – Curious
Explorer. Your deck must include Sapphire cards and can include up to two other ink
types. It can have up to 4 copies of the Moana – Curious Explorer character card, but
no more than 1 copy of any other card.
[Format Coconut] uses the normal multiplayer Disney Lorcana rules with one important
exception:
u The first player to reach 25 lore or more wins the game.
We recommend playing with 3 or 4 players for the best game experience. So, get some
friends together, try it out, and have fun!
