[129] respuesta_debate_o_contexto
There are two different types of abilities at play here. 

LEARN FROM THEIR LOSSES is a triggered ability. When its condition is met ("whenever a robot character is banished") its effect is put into the bag. ("You may banish this item to draw two cards.") When the bag is next resolved, you take the effect out and resolve it. 

MISSING PIECE is a static ability that is always applying. If a Buzz was banished, it gives an alternate method for playing Buzz's Arm. It doesn't ever resolve or get played, it just is always checking and giving you that option when it's appropriate. 
In order to take advantage of that alternate cost, you still need to be able to play the item, which you usually only have the ability to do during your own main phase. (editado)lunes, 15 de junio de 2026, 14:

[152] duda_candidata
The OoOS policy allows players to batch things that the game rules do not allow to be batched - by the CRD alone, nothing can be batched because that’s not how the game works.

However, to legally batch things under the OoOS exception, the order in which the actions being batched are carried out must make no difference to the resolution of the sequence. In this case, that’s not true, because the order of questing will change the lore value gained - so it cannot be a legally batched sequence.

If the batched action is not legal, then it can’t really be considered a “batched action” for the take back policy, and is instead a game error.

(Although ultimately whether or not to allow a take back here doesn’t actually matter, because there was still a GRE either way so the rewind will do the same thing as a take back would and they would still receive the corrective action whether a take back was allowed or not).

For Chris:
OoOS - Out of Order Sequencing (policy in the tournament rules)
CRD - comprehensive rules document

[831] respuesta_debate_o_contexto
1.9.5. If a character or effect would deal damage and that damage is reduced to 0 when modifiers are applied, the character or effect is still considered to “deal” damage. If a character or location is dealt damage and that damage is reduced to 0 when modifiers are applied, the character or location “takes” no damage. 

Example: A player has Merida –Formidable Archer in play. Merida has the ability Steady Aim that reads, “Whenever one of your actions deals damage to an opposing character, deal 2 damage to that character.” The player plays Smash, an action that has the effect, “Deal 3 damage to chosen character.” The player chooses Namaari – Resolute Daughter, a character with Resist +3. Smash deals 3 damage,but Namaari takes 0 damage because of Resist +3. Because Smash is still considered to deal damage, Merida’s Steady Aim triggered ability occurs and is added to the bag.
