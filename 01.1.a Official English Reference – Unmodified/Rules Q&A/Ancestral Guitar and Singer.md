# Ancestral Guitar and Singer

[[Rules Q&A|Rules Q&A index]] · [[Ancestral Guitar no convierte Singer en Singer X|Leer el original en español]]

## Question

[Ancestral Guitar](https://cards.lorcast.io/card/digital/large/crd_2486b7e81831490bb896dcc168a5f31b.avif?1790870346) has an ability called **From the Heart**. Exert the item and pay 1 ink to use it.

> Chosen character gains Singer and counts as having +2 cost to sing songs this turn.

How does the +2 work if the character already has Singer? Who can you choose, and when does the granted keyword matter for other cards?

## Answer

**Ancestral Guitar grants Singer and adds +2 to the cost the chosen character counts as for singing this turn.** If the character already has printed Singer, it keeps that keyword and its number. The +2 still applies. The character’s ink cost stays the same in either case.

### How to work out the cost for singing

Under rules **8.11.1–8.11.2**, Singer N lets a character exert to pay a song’s alternate cost as if its cost were N. That value applies only when singing a song as part of playing it. Rule **8.11.3** adds singing-cost modifiers to that value when the character has a Singer number.

| Character before the ability resolves | Singer after it resolves | Counts as this cost to sing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 ink, Singer 4 | Singer 4 | 6 (4 + 2) |
| 3 ink, no Singer | Singer | 5 (3 + 2) |

Singer doesn’t stack. Under rule **8.1.2**, the printed keyword lasts longer and takes precedence over the temporary grant. The Hyperia City release notes confirm that the +2 still applies. If several effects add to the cost the character counts as for singing, add their bonuses together. Any Singer number stays the same.

### Choosing a character and using the granted keyword

You can choose your own character or an opponent’s character in play, subject to Ward and any other restrictions on choosing. Cards in the deck, hand, discard, and inkwell are outside the Play zone.

For example, give Singer to an opponent’s character, then play [Ernesto de la Cruz – Ruthless Musician](https://cards.lorcast.io/card/digital/large/crd_88b54a70ed1c4bbc96dc741952224222.avif?1790278609). When **Whatever It Takes** resolves, you can choose that character to banish if it still has Singer and is a legal choice.

[Powerline – Megastar](https://cards.lorcast.io/card/digital/large/crd_15bf57ae3c7f4a5cafb9dcb0d8e08182.avif?1790278506) looks for a character card with Singer in your discard, while [Never Too Far Apart](https://cards.lorcast.io/card/digital/large/crd_e47c85d105e64ec3a91cba6dde1658ad.avif?1790870225) looks for such cards among the top nine cards of your deck. The Guitar can only grant Singer to a character in play. If that character leaves play, it loses the granted keyword and the singing bonus as it changes zones.

The granted Singer and the Guitar’s singing bonus expire at the end of the turn. A character keeps any printed Singer it already had.
